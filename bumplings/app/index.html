Backup and privacy

With Android's own backup, everything in Bumplings comes back on a new or reset phone. With a screen lock on the phone, the backup is encrypted with it, so Google can't read it. Moving straight to a new phone works either way.

They go into a Bumplings album. Any photo can also be saved with a tap in 📸 Bump photos. Once in the gallery, a photo is seen by its other apps and backed up with it (to Google Photos, say), so a private one is private there only if you keep it so.
