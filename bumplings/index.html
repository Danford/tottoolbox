Bumplings

A week-by-week pregnancy companion for both of you, on both your phones. No ads, no account, no tracking.

Android

Download for Android

Version 1.20. Or get it inside Tot Toolbox, which keeps it up to date.

iPhone

Open Bumplings
  1. Open this page in Safari.
  2. Tap Open Bumplings, then the Share button.
  3. Choose Add to Home Screen. It opens like an app from then on, and shares with an Android phone.

On iPhone there are no reminders yet: add appointments to your calendar from each one.
