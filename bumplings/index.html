How installing works, and why Android asks

iPhone

Open Bumplings
  1. Open this page in Safari.
  2. Tap Open Bumplings, then the Share button.
  3. Choose Add to Home Screen. It opens like an app from then on.

Sharing works between an iPhone and an Android phone. On iPhone there are no reminders yet: add appointments to your calendar from each one.

Pregnancy to 6 months

Pregnancy, together

Each of you gets it on your own phone, linked by scanning a QR code, and everything stays in step: appointments, the checklist, kick counts, even a contraction timed on one phone shows on the other.

Week by week

  • How far along you are, days to go, and the baby's size
  • What's developing, what's happening in mom's body, and a tip for each of you every week

A checklist with real dates

  • Every usual checkup, screening and vaccine, dated from your due date
  • Getting ready: pediatrician, car seat, hospital bags, who watches older kids

Tools for the big moments

  • Kick counter, with when to call
  • Contraction timer with your provider's rule (5-1-1 by default) and one-tap calls
  • Urgent warning signs from the CDC's Hear Her campaign, before and after birth

Together

  • Bump photo diary, a photo a week
  • Appointments with questions to ask, on both phones
  • Birth plan you fill in together
  • Baby names: you each vote, and names you both love rise to the top

Is it safe?

  • Search foods, drinks, medicines and everyday things for a plain answer from ACOG, CDC and FDA guidance

After the birth

  • Baby log for feeds, diapers and sleep, on both phones
  • Checkups and vaccines for the first six months, for baby and for mom
  • Mood check for both parents, kept private on your own phone

More screens

What's free

Everything, including every safety feature. There is nothing to buy.

Private

No ads, no account, no tracking. What you share between your two phones is end-to-end encrypted, so even we can't read it. Each journal entry and bump photo can be shared or kept just on your phone, and photos start out private.

Privacy policy

Bumplings gives general information based on ACOG, CDC and FDA guidance. It is not medical advice; always follow your own doctor or midwife.
