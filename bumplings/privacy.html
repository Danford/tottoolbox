Bumplings privacy policy

Last updated October 23, 2026

Bumplings is a pregnancy companion made by Danford Enterprises as part of Tot Toolbox. It has no ads, no analytics and no accounts. We do not sell, share or look at what you put in it.

What stays on your phone

Everything you enter is stored on your phone: due date, names, appointments, bump photos, the birth plan, journal entries (mood, symptoms, weight, blood pressure and blood sugar), kick counts, contractions, baby-name votes, checklists, the baby log (feeds, diapers and sleep) and mood check answers.

Journal entries and bump photos can each be shared with the other parent's phone or kept just on yours; new bump photos start out kept just on yours. If you share with the other parent's phone, it holds the same copy of everything else, with when each thing was added and by which parent, except mood checks: of those it gets only the day each was done, so both phones know when the next is due, never the answers or the score.

Backup. Bumplings uses Android's own backup to your Google account, so a new or reset phone gets everything back, private entries and photos included. That backup is Google's service, held in your Google account; we never see it, and with a screen lock on your phone Android encrypts it so Google can't read it either. You can turn it off in Bumplings' Settings ("Back up to my Google account"); after that, Bumplings' data is left out of your backups. Moving straight to a new phone with Android's phone-to-phone transfer brings the data along either way.

Private entries and photos are locked. In the Android app, journal entries and bump photos kept just on your phone, and the key your phones share with, are encrypted on your phone with a key held by Android's keystore, which never leaves the phone and isn't in any backup. A backup or transfer carries them only encrypted. On a new phone they can be unlocked with a backup key you make in Bumplings' Settings and keep yourself, or, while you share with the other parent's phone, with an invite from that phone. Without either, they can't be unlocked, by us or anyone else. Bumplings on the web keeps them in your browser as they are.

Tot Routines on the same phone

If you also use our Tot Routines app on the same phone, it shows the baby on the way beside your other children. To do that it can read, on the phone, a short summary from Bumplings: the due date, the baby's name, the parents' names, the doctor's and hospital's names and numbers, upcoming appointments (what, when and where, and how many questions you saved, not the questions or notes), the checklist, the hospital bag count, the latest kick count, whether a contraction is being timed, baby names you voted for, the last two days of the baby log, the journal's feeling for each of the last two weeks, how many journal entries there are, the newest bump photo (unless you turn that off in Settings), and how this phone's latest mood check went (only whether it looked fine, suggested talking to your provider, or asked for a call that day; never the answers or the score), the day each parent last did a mood check and when the next is due, and the last week of what was added or done (an appointment's title, a checklist item, a name idea, that a journal entry, with its feeling, or a bump photo was added) and by which parent. Nothing in this summary leaves the phone: Tot Routines doesn't save it or share it with anyone, including the other parent's phone. Only apps signed by Tot Toolbox can read it. The journal's notes, symptoms, weight, blood pressure and blood sugar, the birth plan and the other photos are not in it.

Sharing with the other parent's phone

If you choose to share with another phone, your entries and the bump photos you share are encrypted on your phone before they are sent, with a key only your two phones have. Our sync server (sync.tottoolbox.com) stores and relays the encrypted copy so the other phone can receive it. We cannot read it or see the photos. Like any web server, it briefly sees your IP address, which is used only to limit abuse and is not stored with your data. Mood check answers and scores, and journal entries and photos kept just on your phone, are never sent. If you never share, nothing leaves your phone.

Permissions

  • Camera: to scan the invite code shown on the other phone, and to take bump photos when you choose to. Scanning saves no pictures. A bump photo is kept in the app (and shared, encrypted, only if you share).
  • Photos: when you choose a bump photo from your gallery, the app gets only the photo you pick. Bumplings saves a bump photo to your gallery (a Bumplings album) only when you tap Save, or when you turn on saving shared or private bump photos automatically in Settings; both are off at first. Once in your gallery, a photo is handled by your gallery and its backups like any other photo. On Android 9 and earlier, saving asks for leave to write to your phone's storage.
  • Calendar: "Add to my calendar" opens your calendar app with the appointment filled in. Bumplings never reads your calendar.
  • Notifications: weekly updates and appointment reminders, scheduled on your phone.
  • Internet: only for sharing, described above.

Bumplings on the web

Bumplings also runs in a web browser (tottoolbox.com/bumplings/app/), for iPhones. It works the same way: what you enter is stored in your browser on that device, and shared only if you choose to, encrypted as above. Our website host sees your IP address, as any website does, to deliver the page.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app (or, on the web, clearing the site's data) deletes everything on your device. To have the encrypted shared copy deleted from our server too, email support@tottoolbox.com and we will delete it.

Children

Bumplings is meant for adults and is not directed at children.

Medical information

Bumplings gives general information based on ACOG and CDC guidance. It is not medical advice. Always follow your own doctor or midwife.
