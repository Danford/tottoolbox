Tot Compare privacy policy

Last updated October 31, 2026

Tot Compare is made by Danford Enterprises, LLC, doing business as Tot Toolbox, for young children to use with a grown-up. It has no ads, no analytics, no tracking, no accounts, no purchases and no links out of the app.

What it collects

Nothing. The app does not use the internet, so nothing can leave the phone. We never collect anything from children or from you. Its settings stay on the phone, and uninstalling the app deletes them.

Children

The app is made for young children. It collects no personal information from them, and grown-up settings are behind a press and hold.
