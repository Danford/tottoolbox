Toddlers

First words, sounded out

Tap a picture to hear its name and its real sound, tap the word and watch each letter light up as it's sounded out, or slide a finger along it. Counting cards and four little games of opposites are in the same app: pick one, then the pictures to use.

Every word sounds out

  • All 185 words can be sounded out, in time with the voice
  • Letters that make one sound light up together, like the sh in sheep
  • Slide a finger along the word to hear each sound, as slowly or as quickly as your child likes

Thirteen decks of first words

  • Farm, zoo, ocean, birds, bugs, things that go, the state fair, the museum, music, food, home, the sky and colors
  • Big, clear pictures in a warm voice, a man's or a woman's
  • Many animals and machines play their real sound: a cow moos, a fire truck wails

Count from one to ten

  • Ten cards, each with its numeral, its word and that many pictures
  • Tap the pictures one at a time, or tap the card to count them together

Four games of opposites

  • Big and small, tall and short, more and less, fast and slow
  • The voice asks about two pictures; the right one gets a cheer, and a wrong one is gently named

Made for small hands

  • One tap, one word. Swipe or use the big arrows for the next card
  • Nothing to win or lose, and no timers
  • Grown-up settings stay behind a press and hold

What's free

Everything. There is nothing to buy.

Private

No ads, no account, no tracking. Tot Flash Cards never goes online, so it works on a plane or in the car. There are no links out of the app, and its settings stay on the phone.
