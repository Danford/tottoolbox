What you'll want, and when

  1. Bump to six months

    Expecting, then a new baby

    Bumplings is for both of you: where you are this week, every usual checkup with its real date, a kick counter, a contraction timer, and a plain answer to "is it safe?". After the birth it keeps going, with feeds, diapers and sleep on both phones, and the checkups and vaccines of the first six months.

    Bumplings

  2. From age 1

    Days start to have a shape

    Tot Routines helps you build bedtime, mornings and mealtimes that fit your child's age, then hands them a picture chart with their own things and your voice.

    Tot Routines

  3. Toddlers

    Something good to tap

    Tot Flash Cards sounds out every word as the letters light up, counts from one to ten, and has four little games of opposites. It never goes online, and there is nothing in it to buy.

    Tot Flash Cards

  4. Potty time

    When they're ready

    Tot Potty has a check of the signs, a plan for each day, one-tap logging, and a handoff note, so the sitter and grandma do it the way you do.

    Tot Potty

  5. Up to age 7

    School mornings

    Tot Routines grows with them: school mornings, who picks them up, and a balanced plan for each day of the week, tuned to what they love.

    Tot Routines

What free means here

  • No ads. Not in any app, not ever.
  • No account. Nothing to sign up for. Open the app and start.
  • No tracking. The apps have no analytics. What you enter stays on your phone unless you choose to share, and what you share is encrypted so we can't read it.
  • Nothing that keeps a child safe costs money. Warning signs, the contraction timer and when to call the doctor are free for everyone.

Every app is free, and Tot Routines shares with up to 2 phones at no cost. Families who want more phones can become a supporter, which helps keep the apps going.
