How to install, and why Android asks

It takes a couple of minutes, once. Tot Toolbox then installs the other apps and keeps them up to date.

On Android

  1. On your phone, download Tot Toolbox.

    Download for Android

    Version 1.4, under 1 MB.

  2. Open the downloaded file. If it isn't on the screen, look in your notifications or your Downloads folder.

  3. Android says your browser isn't allowed to install apps. Tap Settings, turn on Allow from this source, and go back.

  4. Tap Install, then open Tot Toolbox.

  5. Tap Install beside any app you want. The first time, Android asks the same question about Tot Toolbox, so turn on Allow from this source for it too, and come back.

The words differ a little from phone to phone.

Why it asks you to allow unknown apps

Android trusts Google Play by default and asks before any other app installs something. Our apps aren't on Google Play yet, so they come from this website, and Android wants your OK.

The switch is for one app at a time. Turning it on for your browser lets that browser offer to install a file, and turning it on for Tot Toolbox lets Tot Toolbox install and update our apps. It doesn't open your phone to anything else, and Android still shows you each install before it happens.

If Android or Google Play Protect warns that it doesn't recognize the developer, that's because the apps aren't in Google's store yet. You can choose to install anyway, or stop there and wait.

How you know it's really ours

  • Every app is signed with the same key. Android only installs an update signed with the key the app already has, so nobody else's file can update it.
  • Tot Toolbox checks each download against the fingerprint in our app list before installing it.
  • The apps are published by Danford Enterprises, LLC. If anything looks wrong, email support@tottoolbox.com.

Turning it off afterwards

You can turn the switch off for your browser once Tot Toolbox is in. It's usually in Settings, under Apps, then Special app access, then Install unknown apps. Leave it on for Tot Toolbox, or Android will ask again whenever it installs an update.

On iPhone

Tot Toolbox is an Android app, so iPhones use the web versions instead: Bumplings and Tot Potty and Tot Routines. Open the app's page in Safari, open the app, then use Share and Add to Home Screen. Nothing asks about unknown apps.

Tot Flash Cards aren't on iPhone yet.
