Tot Potty

Potty training, the calm way: a plan for each day, one-tap logging, a timer that learns how often your child goes, stickers and a potty song, and every grown-up who helps on the same page. No ads, no account, no tracking.

iPhone

Open Tot Potty
  1. Open this page in Safari.
  2. Tap Open Tot Potty, then the Share button.
  3. Choose Add to Home Screen. It opens like an app from then on, and shares with Android phones.

On iPhone the timer reminds you only while Tot Potty is open.
