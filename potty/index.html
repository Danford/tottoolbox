How installing works, and why Android asks

iPhone

Open Tot Potty
  1. Open this page in Safari.
  2. Tap Open Tot Potty, then the Share button.
  3. Choose Add to Home Screen. It opens like an app from then on.

It shares with Android phones. On iPhone the timer reminds you only while Tot Potty is open.

Potty training

Potty training, calmer

A plan for each day, one-tap logging, a timer that learns how often your child goes, stickers and a potty song, and everyone who helps on the same page.

A plan for each day

  • A quick check of the signs pediatricians look for
  • Choose the 3-day method, child-led step by step, or potty at set times

One tap to log

  • Pee, poop, accident, a try, a dry check
  • Where you were: home, out, daycare, grandma's

A timer that learns

  • Starts again from every pee and rests while your child sleeps
  • After a few days it suggests the right interval

For your child

  • Sticker chart and prize
  • A two-minute sit timer with a song and bubbles
  • Handwashing step by step

When it's hard

  • Guides for poop worries, accidents, power struggles, going backwards, outings and more
  • When to call the doctor, with a one-tap call

Everyone on the same page

  • Share with a partner, grandparents, the nanny or the sitter, Android or iPhone
  • A handoff note for sitters and daycare
