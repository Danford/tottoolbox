Tot Potty privacy policy

Last updated October 26, 2026

Tot Potty is a potty-training app for parents and the grown-ups who help them, made by Danford Enterprises as part of Tot Toolbox. It has no ads, no analytics and no accounts. We do not sell, share or look at what you put in it.

What stays on your phone

Everything you enter is stored on your phone: your name, each child's name, birthday and the words your family uses, what you log (pees, poops, accidents, tries, dry checks, naps and nights, with the time, where, who started it and any note), the potty timer, stickers and prizes, the plan you chose and its ticks, the readiness check, the gear list, notes for sitters, and the doctor's name and number.

Backup. Tot Potty uses Android's own backup to your Google account, so a new or reset phone gets everything back. That backup is Google's service, held in your Google account; we never see it, and with a screen lock on your phone Android encrypts it so Google can't read it either. You can turn it off in Tot Potty's Settings. The key your phones share with is encrypted with a key held by Android's keystore, which never leaves the phone and isn't in any backup; on a new phone you scan an invite from another phone to share again.

Tot Routines on the same phone

If you also use our Tot Routines app on the same phone, it shows how potty training is going on each child's day. To do that it can read, on the phone, a short summary from Tot Potty: each child's name, birthday, your family's words, the approach and step, the last eight days' counts (in the potty, accidents, tries, dry checks, how many they asked for), the time of the latest pee or accident, the potty timer, the sticker count and prize, the dry streak, the last week's nights, milestones and prizes, and the names of the grown-ups who gave prizes. Nothing in this summary leaves the phone. Only apps signed by Tot Toolbox can read it. Notes, where things happened, and who logged each thing are not in it.

Sharing with other grown-ups' phones

If you choose to share with other phones (a partner, grandparent, nanny or sitter), everything above except this phone's own settings is encrypted on your phone before it is sent, with a key only your family's phones have. Our sync server (sync.tottoolbox.com) stores and relays the encrypted copy so the other phones can receive it. We cannot read it. Like any web server, it briefly sees your IP address, which is used only to limit abuse and is not stored with your data. Anyone who scans an invite joins, so show invites only to people you trust. If you never share, nothing leaves your phone.

Permissions

  • Camera: only to scan the invite code shown on another phone. No pictures are saved.
  • Notifications and alarms: the potty timer's reminders, scheduled on your phone. Exact alarms, if you allow them, make the reminder come on time.
  • Vibrate: a short buzz when you log and when the timer is due.
  • Internet: only for sharing, described above.

Tot Potty on the web

Tot Potty also runs in a web browser (tottoolbox.com/potty/app/), for iPhones. It works the same way: what you enter is stored in your browser on that device, and shared only if you choose to, encrypted as above. Our website host sees your IP address, as any website does, to deliver the page.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app (or, on the web, clearing the site's data) deletes everything on your device. To have the encrypted shared copy deleted from our server too, email support@tottoolbox.com and we will delete it.

Children

Tot Potty is for adults. Its kid mode shows a child their sticker chart, a sit timer and handwashing steps on a grown-up's phone; it collects nothing from the child.

Medical information

Tot Potty gives general information based on American Academy of Pediatrics and NHS guidance. It is not medical advice. Ask your child's doctor about anything that worries you.
