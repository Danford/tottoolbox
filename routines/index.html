Tot RoutinesFree visual routine charts for ages 1 to 7

Routines for ages 1 to 7, a picture chart they follow, and a plan for the week.

For the grown-ups looking after a child aged 1 to 7: mornings, bedtime, mealtimes and school days, each made for your child's age, with what's realistic to expect and how to make each step stick.

How installing works, and why Android asks

iPhone

Open Tot Routines
  1. Open this page in Safari.
  2. Tap Open Tot Routines, then the Share button.
  3. Choose Add to Home Screen. It opens like an app from then on.

Add it to your Home Screen before you start: an iPhone can clear what a website keeps after 7 days without a visit. It shares with Android phones. There are no reminders or spoken steps yet, and videos can be up to 5 minutes.

What's in it

Routines that fit their age

  • Start from bedtime, a school morning, after school, nap time, mealtimes, leaving the house or clean-up
  • Each step shows what's realistic at your child's age and how to teach it
  • A reminder before a routine starts

A picture chart they follow

  • Photos of their own things: their toothbrush, their pajamas, their bed
  • Your own voice saying each step, or let the phone say it
  • Print it for the wall

A balanced week

  • A few planned activities for each day, never a timetable: the rest is free play
  • Tuned to what your child loves
  • School days with when they leave, when they're home and who picks them up

The whole family

  • Each child's day on their own page, and everyone's day together, by time
  • A journal of photos, videos and notes

Share with the other grown-ups

  • Link another grown-up's phone by scanning a code on yours
  • See every phone in the family, and remove one at any time

With Bumplings and Tot Potty

  • Expecting? Tot Routines shows the baby on the way beside your children
  • Each child's day shows how potty training is going

What's free

Every routine, picture chart and week plan, and the journal.

Sharing with up to 2 phones, with journal videos at 720p.

Sharing with more phones, and videos at the best quality, comes with Supporter.

Private

No ads, no account, no tracking. Everything stays on your phone unless you choose to share. What you share is end-to-end encrypted, with a key only your family's phones hold, so even we can't read it.

Privacy policy

Tot Routines gives general information based on pediatric guidance. It is not medical advice; if something worries you, ask your child's doctor.
