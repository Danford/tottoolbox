What it costs

Everything is free up to 2 phones, with no trial and no ads. Only Tot Routines has anything to pay for, and only when your family wants more phones or the best journal videos.

How to add a phone

  1. Open Tot Routines and tap Share with another grown-up. Your first two phones are free.

  2. When you're ready for a third, tap Invite another grown-up. Tot Routines shows you the choices.

  3. Pay on the secure payment page, go back to Tot Routines, and invite the next phone.

You can also start from Photos and privacy, at the bottom of the home screen. On iPhone, open Tot Routines from your Home Screen, not from a Safari tab, so the plan goes to your family.

Good to know

  • Private. What you share is encrypted on your phones, with a key only your family's phones hold. There are still no ads, no account and no tracking.
  • Easy to stop. Cancel any time from the phone that paid. It lasts to the end of what you paid for, nothing more is charged, and phones already sharing keep sharing.
  • It helps. Supporters keep the apps going, and keep them free for every family.
  • Nothing else changes. Bumplings, Tot Potty and Tot Flash Cards have nothing to buy, and nothing that keeps a child safe is ever behind a payment.
